Why this exists

Construction software is closed at exactly the layer that should be open. The file formats are ISO standards, the building codes are public law, and the carbon factors are published research — yet reading an IFC file, checking a plan against a code, or pricing a model's embodied carbon still means a paid seat in someone's suite, and an answer you cannot audit.

So we build in two layers. Underneath: a small, dependency-free open toolchain that reads models, checks drawings, prices carbon and answers questions with a citation back to the source line. On top: commercial products that carry Vietnamese regulation — NĐ 06/2021, QCVN 06:2022, NĐ 123/2020, Luật Đấu thầu 2023 — as data, not as a consultant's slide deck.

The open layer is not marketing for the closed one. It is the part we think should never have been closed.
